# CHB-MIT derived teaching subset
Source: Guttag, J. (2010). CHB-MIT Scalp EEG Database (version 1.0.0). PhysioNet.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.13026/C2K01R
Dataset: https://physionet.org/content/chbmit/1.0.0/
License: Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0:
https://physionet.org/content/chbmit/view-license/1.0.0/

NeuroSim Lab extracted eight channels from chb01/chb01_03.edf, elapsed
[2960, 3080) seconds. The full EDF is not distributed here. The NPZ retains
lossless digital samples, not normalized or filtered voltage. JSON supplies
EDF calibration, source and artifact checksums, preparation tool versions,
source links and exact annotation. The copied summary is the official source
annotation. SOURCE-SHA256.txt preserves its official EDF checksum entry.
See scripts/prepare_eeg.py and docs/phase4-report.md for reproduction and limits.
